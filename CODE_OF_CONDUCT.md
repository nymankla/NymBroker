# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful community for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Share criticism and differing opinions thoughtfully.
- Focus discussions on what is best for the project and its community.
- Accept feedback and help keep interactions welcoming.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, personal attacks, and unwelcome sexual attention are not acceptable. Public or private conduct that substantially disrupts the project community may also be addressed.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. To report behavior that violates this code, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub rather than raising it in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, or restrict participation, depending on the circumstances.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.
